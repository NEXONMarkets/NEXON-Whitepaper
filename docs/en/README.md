## What NEXON is

NEXON is a value-connection network where AI agents translate between capital markets, digital assets, and real-world spending — turning what you mean into what gets settled.

Three markets hold the world's value, and none of them can read the others. Equities speak in ownership and future cash flow, settle T+2, and stop at national borders. Digital assets speak in liquidity and composability, settle in seconds, and stop at the edge of the real world. Travel, hotels and retail speak in access — being able to use something, at a time, in a place. Same value. Three incompatible languages.

Turning a position into a hotel room still takes four systems, four identity checks and a week. Every step is a human manually translating one language into another. That was tolerable when software could only move value. It stopped being tolerable the moment software could understand it.

NEXON puts agents in that gap. You state an Intent; agents parse it, route it across all three markets, and settle it end to end — down to the Real Leg. The connection does not run on bridges. It runs on agents.

NEXON is an independent, community-initiated project built within the NEX ecosystem. It is not an official NEX product, and neither XO nor EXON is an exchange token.

- **Twenty minutes:** read [Part II](02-the-translator/README.md). Everything else expands it.
- **Technical readers:** [Part III](03-architecture/README.md), then [Security & Risk](07-security-and-risk/README.md).

- **Questions about the two assets:** [Two Assets, Two Jobs](05-tokenomics/two-assets-two-jobs.md) first, then [Open Parameters](open-parameters/README.md) for what is not yet decided.
- **Status badges** appear throughout and mean exactly one thing each:

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Live` | Deployed and usable today. |
| `In development` | Being built; behaviour described is the target, not a claim of delivery. |
| `Roadmap` | Planned; not built. Never read as available. |
| `Design Target` | A design choice made for now that may change before release. |
| `Open` | Deliberately undecided; listed in Open Parameters with an owner. |
