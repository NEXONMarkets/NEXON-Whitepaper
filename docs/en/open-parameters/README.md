## Closure rule

An Open field must remain visibly Open. A later written decision should identify the approving party, evidence, effective scope, version and products affected. It must then update the relevant authority registry, both language trees, Wiki, user disclosures and validation tests together.

Narrative positioning cannot close an economic field. A product mockup cannot close a custody or legal field. A third-party announcement cannot close a NEXON operating field unless the responsible relationship is documented. Conflicting older materials remain historical and cannot be used to complete a blank.

Open does not mean unimportant or assumed. It means the project has deliberately preserved a decision boundary so readers can tell confirmed mechanism from future design.
