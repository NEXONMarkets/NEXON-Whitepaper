## The closure rule

An Open field must **stay visibly Open**. A later written decision names the approving party, the evidence, the effective scope, the version and the products affected. It then updates the authority registry, both language trees, the Wiki, user disclosures and the validation tests **together**.

Three things that cannot close a field:

* **A narrative position cannot close an economic field.**
* **A product mockup cannot close a custody or legal field.**
* **A third-party announcement cannot close a NEXON operating field** unless the responsible relationship is documented.

Conflicting older material stays historical and is never used to complete a blank.

**Open does not mean unimportant, and it does not mean assumed.** It means the project has deliberately preserved a decision boundary, so a reader can tell confirmed mechanism from future design.
