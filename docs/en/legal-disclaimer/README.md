# Legal Disclaimer

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Read this before relying on anything in this paper.** It describes a proposed protocol design. It is not investment advice, not an offer or solicitation, and not a promise that any capability in it will be built or made available to you. Version 1 deliberately contains no token parameters.
{% endhint %}

This paper describes a value-connection network in which agents translate between capital markets, digital assets and real-world spending. It explains a design. It sells nothing.

## Not investment advice

Nothing here is financial, legal, tax or investment advice, and none of it is addressed to your circumstances. It says how a protocol is meant to work, not whether taking part in it is suitable for you. Take your own advice before acting on any of it.

## Not an offer or solicitation

This paper is not a prospectus, an offering document or a solicitation of any kind, in any jurisdiction. It offers nothing for sale and invites no one to buy any asset, right or participation. Any early participation round (`Open`, [OP-23](../open-parameters/README.md)) would run under its own terms and would not be governed by this document. An exchange listing is not promised here, and none should be inferred.

## Forward-looking statements and status badges

A capability that does not yet exist is described here as design intent, not as fact about a product. Three badges fix how such statements read:

- `Roadmap` — described, not built. Not a commitment to build it, as described or at all.
- `Design Target` — a value the current design assumes; it may change before Version 2 without notice.
- `Open` — no value chosen; the number, name or party is listed in [Open Parameters](../open-parameters/README.md).

`In development` promises no completion date. No capability in Version 1 is described as live.

## Jurisdictions

Where NEXON's capabilities will be offered, and to whom, is an Open Parameter (`Open`, [OP-21](../open-parameters/README.md)). Until it is fixed, assume that some or all of what this paper describes may be unavailable, restricted or prohibited where you are.

## Nature of XO and EXON

XO and EXON are utility assets. XO is used to obtain agent execution capacity and governance standing; EXON is used for settlement and circulation. Neither represents equity, debt, or a right to the profits or distributions of any entity. A formal legal opinion will be issued as required.

That characterisation is the project's own, not a legal opinion; [Compliance & Legal Posture](../08-compliance/README.md) sets out who executes each sensitive capability.

## Relationship to NEX

NEXON is an independent, community-initiated project built within the NEX ecosystem. It is not an official NEX product, and neither XO nor EXON is an exchange token. Statements about NEX, its licences or its products are NEX's to make, and this paper makes none on its behalf.

## Versions and canonical language

This is Version 1, the mechanism release. Supply, allocation, vesting, emission and any relationship between the two assets are Open Parameters, filled in Version 2 without rewriting the chapters that cite them; Version 2 may also correct, restate or withdraw anything in Version 1. The English edition is canonical. The Simplified Chinese edition is a localisation; where the two differ, the English governs, except for the sentences the [Glossary](../glossary/README.md) identifies as written in Chinese first.

## Sources and notes

Version 1 cites no external statistics, market data or third-party research and makes no numerical claim about any market. Sources will be listed here as later versions introduce them.
