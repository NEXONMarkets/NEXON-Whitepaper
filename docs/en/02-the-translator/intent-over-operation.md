# Express Intent, Not Operations

An Intent records the desired outcome, amount, deadline, acceptable slippage, eligible venues and approval mode. Before execution, the runtime presents a route with each leg, quote, responsible executor and expiry.

## Five steps

1. **Parse.** Convert natural language into a structured request and ask for clarification when material information is missing.
2. **Route.** Produce a complete proposal; nothing moves yet.
3. **Policy Check.** Validate identity, jurisdiction, balances, user-set limits and product-specific requirements. For a staking order, this includes the 28% EXON reserve check.
4. **Execute.** Grant bounded, time-limited authority for one approved leg at a time.
5. **Land.** Record the final outcome. If a leg fails, stop and follow the disclosed rollback or dispute path.

The route can only use the assets and permissions displayed to the user. XO does not grant an agent execution authority, and EXON is not charged as a universal route fee under the approved economic model.
