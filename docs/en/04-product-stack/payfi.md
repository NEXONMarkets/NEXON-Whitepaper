# PayFi — the Agent’s Hand

PayFi is the controlled execution surface for approved payment or conversion instructions. It applies account eligibility, quote expiry, venue limits, scoped permissions, receipts and rollback handling.

Its product fees, where applicable, must be disclosed under the relevant service terms. The approved tokenomics does not define EXON as a universal PayFi fee or settlement token, and holding XO does not grant an execution limit. Staking rewards and redemption burns remain inside the separate Staking Platform.

This boundary prevents a product feature from silently changing token demand assumptions or turning a staking balance into payment authority.
