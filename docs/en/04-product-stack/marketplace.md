# Marketplace — The Real-Consumption Leg

**Status** · `Roadmap`

The NEXON Marketplace is planned as the ecosystem surface where a financial route can become a right of use: travel, a hotel stay, goods or services. It represents the “Digital to Real” half of the project tagline. Its job is not finished when payment settles. Completion requires evidence that the supplier accepted and fulfilled the order.

## From route to order

A marketplace leg begins with an approved intent and fresh supplier data. The preview should identify the item or booking, inventory, total cost, accepted payment method, conversion if required, cancellation window, delivery conditions and the supplier responsible. If price or availability changes before approval, the route must update rather than substitute silently.

After execution, the route records at least two states:

1. **Payment or reservation state** — submitted, authorized, settled, failed or refunded.
2. **Fulfillment state** — confirmed, delivered, consumed, cancelled or disputed.

A settled payment with no delivery is not a successful real-world leg.

## Supplier responsibility

Merchants, hotels, travel operators and service providers control their own inventory and fulfillment. Their terms govern delivery, cancellation, refund and consumer protection. NEXON may coordinate the experience and preserve the receipts, but it should not imply that a token transfer overrides a supplier obligation or guarantees availability.

Supplier onboarding should include identity and business verification, product and jurisdiction checks, data-security requirements, service terms and an escalation path. Ratings or social reputation may inform discovery, but they do not replace diligence or a formal dispute process.

## Payments and token boundaries

The approved Tokenomics paper does not define EXON as a current universal Marketplace payment asset. It also does not define XO as merchant collateral. Accepted payment methods, conversion, spreads, fees, settlement timing and refunds must be specified by the responsible product and provider before the user approves an order.

EXON's broader trading, payment, exchange, fee and consumption function is part of its Roadmap Circulation Engine role. It becomes an actual product utility only where support and terms are published. Staking rewards and redemption burns remain inside the separate Staking Platform mechanism.

## Data and privacy

Real-world orders may require names, addresses, travel dates or identity information. The marketplace should share only the fields required by the supplier, explain retention, and separate private order data from anything a user elects to share in the Social App. A receipt can prove route history without making personal travel or purchase details public.

## Launch gates

Before availability, the Marketplace requires supported categories and jurisdictions, supplier contracts, inventory and pricing interfaces, payment and refund terms, privacy controls, fraud prevention, customer support and dispute procedures. Specific suppliers, dates, fees and accepted assets remain Open.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Supplier inventory, pricing and fulfillment can change. NEXON's Roadmap marketplace does not guarantee delivery, refund, product quality or the future acceptance or value of XO or EXON.
{% endhint %}
