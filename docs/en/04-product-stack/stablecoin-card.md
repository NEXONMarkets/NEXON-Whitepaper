# Stablecoin Card

**Status** · `Roadmap`

A Stablecoin Card, if launched, would be issued and operated by a responsible licensed third party. That party—not NEXON—would define eligibility, accepted funding assets, conversion, fees, custody, limits, refunds and cardholder protections.

The approved tokenomics does not specify EXON as card settlement fuel or XO as card collateral. Card economics must remain separate from Staking Platform rewards and EXON redemption burns.

Availability depends on issuer coverage, KYC/AML requirements and local law. No issuer, launch date or jurisdiction is committed in this paper.
