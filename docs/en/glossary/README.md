## Usage discipline

"Guide price," "APY," "ROI" and "static payback" describe **parameters or conditional arithmetic**. None of them states a guaranteed outcome. "Value Anchor" does not mean a stable price or a legal claim. "Circulation Engine" does not mean EXON is accepted for every payment or fee.

Where narrative and mechanism terms appear together, **state both levels**. The preferred form:

> XO is the Value Anchor and the current Staking Principal Token; broader rights require separately published rules.
>
> EXON is the Circulation Engine and the current Core Value Token for spot, release, purchase, checks and redemption burns; broader application utility requires product terms.
