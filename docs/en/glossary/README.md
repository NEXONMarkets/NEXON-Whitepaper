## Usage rules

The terms “guide price”, “APY”, “ROI” and “static payback” describe parameters or conditional arithmetic. None states a guaranteed outcome. “Value Anchor” does not mean a stable price or legal claim. “Circulation Engine” does not mean EXON is accepted for every payment or fee. “Roadmap” must remain adjacent to planned product capabilities until current availability is documented.

Where narrative and mechanism terms appear together, state both levels. The preferred form is: “XO is the Value Anchor and current Staking Principal Token; broader rights remain Roadmap,” and “EXON is the Circulation Engine and current Core Value Token for spot, release, purchase, checks and redemption burns; broader application utility remains Roadmap.”
