# Compliance & Legal Posture

NEXON is an independent, community-initiated project built within the NEX ecosystem; it is not an official NEX product. XO and EXON are NEXON project assets, not NEX platform tokens. A name, integration or account connection must not imply authorship, approval, liability or principal protection that has not been formally documented.

## Current operating separation

NEX Main Exchange/CEX is the compliant spot layer for EXON spot activity, IEO and release display. The Staking Platform separately applies single-token staking, term weighting, dynamic rewards and redemption. Exchange licences, controls or disclosures cannot be used to imply regulatory approval of a staking return.

Each Roadmap product requires its own responsible-party analysis. PayFi orchestration does not itself provide every underlying regulated service. Wallet custody depends on the implemented custody model. Prediction-market access depends on the independent protocol and jurisdiction. Marketplace suppliers remain responsible for goods and services. A Stablecoin Card depends on an eligible licensed issuer/operator.

## Four disclosure classes

| Class | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| Approved current parameter | State the value, source, effective version and adjustability |
| Conditional illustration | State every price and execution assumption next to the result and disclose principal-loss risk |
| Roadmap capability | Label it Roadmap and name the specifications, operators and eligibility still required |
| Open or unpublished field | Keep it visibly Open; do not estimate or complete it from a conflicting document |

Market outcomes form a separate category: never guarantee price, liquidity, return, payback, fulfillment or recovery.

## Product and jurisdiction gates

KYC, AML, sanctions, custody, exchange execution, payment, card issuance, financial promotion, data processing and any regulated real-world activity must follow the applicable terms of the responsible party. Availability can depend on residence, citizenship, account status, product classification, merchant category and local law.

Before a route is approved, the user should be told which entity executes each leg, which terms apply, what data is shared, how costs are calculated, whether the action is reversible and which dispute path controls. One general ecosystem disclaimer cannot replace product-specific disclosure.

## Token-language discipline

XO's Value Anchor role does not establish equity, debt, deposit status, current voting rights or an income guarantee. EXON's Circulation Engine role does not make it a current universal payment or fee token. Future rights and utilities require published product terms and jurisdictional review.

Likewise, “fixed supply,” “programmatic purchase” and “burn” must not be presented as price assurance. The 200% Base APY and worked examples are mechanism parameters and conditional arithmetic, not expected or promised returns.

## Data and AI

Roadmap AI-Native PayFi and Social features may process financial intent, identity references, transaction context and communications. Product design must define lawful basis, data minimization, retention, deletion, cross-border transfer, provider access and user rights. Model output should be treated as assistance, not as legal, tax or investment advice.
