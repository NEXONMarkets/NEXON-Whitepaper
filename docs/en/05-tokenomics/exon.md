# EXON — Core Value Token

EXON is the sole core value token and spot-market asset in the approved model. It can be acquired in early rounds, through the automatic 28% build, or on the secondary market. From TGE, releases appear in the NEX spot account and can be held or traded.

Three mechanisms create demand or constrain available supply:

1. Every full order routes 28% to an EXON purchase at the prevailing price and places the acquired amount in Treasury Liquidity.
2. The fuel-reserve check requires EXON equal in value to 28% of a staking order before it opens; the asset remains in the user's account.
3. Reward redemption permanently burns an equivalent amount of EXON at 30%, 15% or 0%, depending on the selected release speed. A reserve shortfall requires a spot purchase before the burn.

EXON has a fixed-supply regime with gradual release. The approved paper does not state its numerical total supply, complete allocation or initial float. Those fields remain open and no estimate is presented here.

The TGE `1.0 U` figure is a guide price, not a guaranteed execution price. The early `0.1 U` price and later market price do not imply a promised multiple.

{% hint style="danger" %}
EXON may be volatile or illiquid. Programmatic demand and burns do not guarantee price appreciation, an exit price or recoverability of principal.
{% endhint %}
