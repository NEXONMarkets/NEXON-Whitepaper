# EXON — Circulation Engine

EXON also has two definitions that align without overlapping.

In the NEXON narrative, EXON is the **Circulation Engine**, connecting digital finance to the exchange, experiences, payment, fees and consumption. In the current mechanism, EXON is the **Core Value Token**: subscribed in the private sale, paid out as dynamic rewards, listed on spot, released daily, bought as fuel, burned on static withdrawal, exchanged for X Points.

## Name and narrative role <a href="#name-and-narrative-role" id="name-and-narrative-role"></a>

`EX` comes from exchange and experience. `ON` is circulation switched on. EXON is **the side of the two-asset model that keeps moving**: value that can be priced, exchanged and used as the ecosystem unfolds.

## Two ways in: subscribe, or earn it <a href="#the-only-route-the-private-sale" id="the-only-route-the-private-sale"></a>

**There are two ways to hold EXON: subscribe in the private sale, or earn it — referral and leadership rewards are paid in EXON. Nobody can buy it on the market.** The sale: Subscription price 0.1 USDT, three tiers of 1,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 USDT with 10,000 / 1,000 / 500 allocations — 11,500 in total, closed once sold out. Each subscription is paired with an XO stake at 3:1: 1,000 USDT subscribed pairs with a 333 USDT stake, the subscription earns the release, the stake earns the settlement, and both income lines start together.

EXON supply is 1 billion; the private sale offers only 200 million, 20% of supply. Listing is at 1.0 USDT, ten times the subscription price; from listing day EXON is released daily for 1,095 days, one payout every 12 hours, each payout sellable on NEX.

## Sell only, never buy <a href="#sell-only-never-buy" id="sell-only-never-buy"></a>

> **Nobody can buy EXON: you subscribe, or you earn it. No hot money in and out, no round-tripping — one direction only.**

* The NEX secondary market lists sell orders only, never buy orders, and EXON is not listed on decentralized exchanges — there is no "buy EXON" route anywhere in the market.
* EXON sits in three places: the accounts of private-sale participants, the accounts of leaders earning dynamic rewards, and the fuel of stakers. Fuel EXON can only be burned.
* Selling carries a 5% fee.
* The release schedule is fixed: 200 million released daily over 1,095 days, 182.6k a day, fully predictable.

## Three current supply-and-demand mechanisms <a href="#three-current-supply-and-demand-mechanisms" id="three-current-supply-and-demand-mechanisms"></a>

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="① Fuel-wallet purchase" %}
28% of every staking deposit buys EXON at 1 USDT each as fuel. Deposit 10,000 USDT and the fuel balance shows 2,800 EXON.

Fuel only ever fills: no withdrawals, no transfers, burned only when static yield is withdrawn. **Every deposit buys.**
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="② Burn on withdrawal" %}
Withdrawing static yield burns fuel EXON according to the settlement speed: immediate settlement burns 30% of the amount withdrawn, 30-day 20%, 60-day 10%. Withdraw 10,000 USDT of rewards immediately and, at 1.0 USDT, 3,000 EXON burn.

Static yield burns on every withdrawal, and what is burned leaves circulation for good; referral and leadership rewards are paid in EXON and burn no fuel. Each level buys X Points with EXON every day (V1 and V2 use 0). **Every static withdrawal burns; every day's X Points consume.**
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="③ Burn scenarios" %}
With all rewards withdrawn immediately and EXON at 1.0 USDT: 10 million USDT staked burns 12k – 36k EXON a day; 50 million burns 60k – 180k; 100 million burns 120k – 360k. The early-bird release is 182.6k a day.

At 100 million USDT staked, a single day burns up to 360k EXON — 2.0× the daily release — and a year's burn approaches two years of release.
{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}
