# EXON — Circulation Engine

EXON also carries two definitions that align without saying the same thing.

In the NEXON narrative, EXON is the **Circulation Engine**, connecting digital finance with exchange, experience, payments, fees and consumption. In the approved mechanism, EXON is the **Core Value Token** and public spot asset, used for release, purchase, balance checks and redemption burns.

## Name and narrative role

`EX` comes from exchange and experience. `ON` signals circulation enabled. EXON is **the side of the dual-asset model that is always in motion**: value that can be priced, exchanged and eventually used as the ecosystem opens up.

"Circulation Engine" does not mean every NEXON action costs EXON today. It establishes no universal settlement asset. Each future payment, fee, exchange or consumption use needs an available product and published terms.

## Current acquisition and market role

EXON can be acquired three ways: **early-round subscription, the automatic 28% build attached to a qualifying order, and secondary-market purchase.**

From TGE, released EXON appears in the NEX spot account and can be held or traded. The early price is `0.1 U`; the TGE guide price is `1.0 U`.

## Three current supply-and-demand mechanisms

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="① Order build" %}
Every qualifying order routes `B = 0.28 × P` into an EXON purchase at the prevailing price, and the EXON acquired moves into Treasury Liquidity.

Because it executes at the prevailing price, **the quantity of tokens acquired depends on the execution price**, not on the U amount alone.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="② Fuel balance check" %}
Before a staking order opens, the user's account must hold EXON with a U value of `F = 0.28 × P`.

That balance **stays in the user's account**. Under the approved mechanism it is not a fee, a transfer, a lock or a burn. When the market price moves, the quantity needed to satisfy the same U value moves with it.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="③ Redemption burn" %}
Pending rewards are released through T+0, 30D or 60D. Those lanes permanently destroy an equivalent 30%, 15% or 0% of EXON and release a net 70%, 85% or 100%.

If the user does not hold enough EXON for a burn-bearing lane, a market purchase is required before redemption can complete.
{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}

The burn attaches to **the selected redemption path**. It does not stand for a continuous, discretionary market-support programme.

## Fixed supply, and the number not yet published

The approved paper describes EXON as **fixed supply with gradual, scheduled release**. It does not disclose the numerical total supply, the full allocation, the initial circulating amount or concentration. "Fixed supply" cannot be expanded into an invented figure.

Early allocations release linearly over **1,095 days and 2,190 twelve-hour epochs**. Later rounds begin releasing T+1 under round-specific terms.

## Long-term utility (Roadmap)

EXON's broader trading, payment, exchange, fee and consumption roles belong to the **long-term direction**. Before a product uses EXON, its terms have to state:

| What must be stated | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Whether EXON is required, optional, or converted before settlement | Whether the user actually needs to hold it |
| Price source, quote expiry, liquidity source and slippage treatment | What the user actually pays |
| Recipient and purpose of any fee | Where the money goes |
| Custody and refund handling | Who holds the asset when something breaks |
| Supported jurisdictions and user eligibility | Who can use it |
| Merchant, issuer or other responsible executor | Who to go to |
| Whether the use changes supply or only transfers ownership | Its real effect on the token economy |

That table applies to AI-Native PayFi, the Marketplace, the Stablecoin Card and social value interactions alike. **This paper assumes no single fee schedule and no single payment route.**
