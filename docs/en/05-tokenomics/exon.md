# EXON — Circulation Engine

EXON also has two aligned but distinct descriptions. In the NEXON narrative, EXON is the **Circulation Engine** connecting digital finance with exchange, experience, payments, fees and consumption. In the current approved mechanism, EXON is the **Core Value Token** and public spot asset used in release, purchase, balance-check and redemption-burn operations.

The current functions are confirmed. Broader application utility is Roadmap.

## Name and narrative role

`EX` evokes exchange and experience. `ON` signals circulation enabled. EXON is intended to be the actively moving side of the two-asset model: value can be priced, exchanged and eventually used across ecosystem applications while XO carries the long-term anchor role.

“Circulation Engine” does not mean that every NEXON action currently charges EXON. It does not establish a universal settlement asset or guarantee demand. Each future payment, fee, exchange or consumption use requires an available product and published terms.

## Current acquisition and market role

EXON may be acquired in early rounds, through the automatic 28% build attached to a qualifying order, or through the secondary market. From TGE, released EXON appears in the NEX spot account and may be held or traded subject to account access, market price and liquidity.

The TGE `1.0 U` value is a guide price. The early `0.1 U` price and later market prices do not imply a guaranteed multiple, execution price or exit.

## Three current demand and supply mechanisms

### Order build

Every qualifying order routes `B = 0.28 × P` to an EXON spot purchase at the prevailing price. The acquired EXON moves into Treasury Liquidity. The token quantity therefore depends on actual execution price.

### Fuel-balance check

The user must hold EXON with U-value `F = 0.28 × P` before the staking order opens. This amount remains in the user's account. It is not a fee, transfer, lock or burn under the approved mechanism. Market movement can change the quantity required to satisfy the U-value check.

### Reward-redemption burn

Pending rewards can be released through T+0, 30D or 60D. Those choices permanently destroy an equivalent 30%, 15% or 0% amount of EXON and release a net 70%, 85% or 100% reward. If the user lacks the required EXON amount for a burn-bearing lane, a market purchase may be required before redemption can complete.

The burn is conditional on the selected redemption path. It does not imply continuous discretionary market support or guarantee that supply reduction will exceed vesting releases or sales.

## Fixed-supply regime and unpublished number

The approved paper describes EXON as fixed supply with gradual release. It does not disclose the numerical total supply, full allocation, initial circulating amount or concentration. “Fixed supply” must not be expanded into an invented number.

Early allocations release over 1,095 days in 2,190 twelve-hour epochs. Later-round releases begin T+1 under round-specific terms. Release into a spot account does not guarantee a buyer, price or ability to exit without slippage.

## Roadmap utility

EXON's intended trading, payment, exchange, fee and consumption roles are **Roadmap** beyond the current functions above. Before a product uses EXON, its terms should state:

- whether EXON is required, optional or converted before settlement;
- price source, quote expiry, liquidity source and slippage treatment;
- recipient and purpose of any fee;
- custody and refund handling;
- supported jurisdictions and user eligibility;
- merchant, issuer or other responsible executor;
- whether the use changes supply or only transfers ownership.

This applies to AI-Native PayFi, Marketplace, Stablecoin Card and social value interactions. The whitepaper does not assume one universal fee schedule or payment route.
