# XO — Staking Principal Token

XO is the principal carrier for interest-bearing staking in the approved economic model. A user acquires it from Treasury at a U-denominated price managed by the protocol. It circulates within the system and is independent of the public secondary market.

For a complete U order, 72% becomes staking principal:

```text
S = 0.72 × P
```

`S` is the present-value/reward base used in both static staking calculations and dynamic reward accounting. The 28% EXON build is separate and does not increase this base.

XO's defined job is intentionally narrow. It is neither NEX's platform token nor EXON's substitute. No numerical XO supply or external listing plan is provided in the approved paper.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Calling XO a principal carrier does not make the principal protected. Early exit from the 30-day term deducts 10%–15% of `S`, and all participation remains exposed to protocol, market, liquidity, custody and legal risk.
{% endhint %}
